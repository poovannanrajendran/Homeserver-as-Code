- Lloyd's/London Market professionals (underwriters, brokers, actuaries, risk managers)
- Insurance AI and insurtech followers
- AI practitioners and product builders

1. Lloyd's market insight (news, trends, analysis)

- No AI clichés ("in today's fast-paced world", "game-changing", "revolutionise").
